## Science
- Combinations: PCMB (Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics, Biology) and PCMC (Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics, Computer Science).
- Integrated Coaching: NEET Coaching (for PCMB), JEE Coaching (for PCMB & PCMC), and KCET Coaching (for PCMB & PCMC).
- Languages: Mandatory I Language English; II Language Kannada, Hindi, Sanskrit, or French (North Campus offers Kannada and Hindi).
- Focus: Strong conceptual foundations, analytical reasoning, and practical laboratory learning for engineering, medicine, and pure sciences.

## Commerce
- Combinations:
  * North Campus (Yelahanka): CEBA (Computer Science, Economics, Business Studies, Accountancy).
  * Whitefield Campus: CEBA, SEBA (Statistics, Economics, Business Studies, Accountancy), and MEBA (Mathematics, Economics, Business Studies, Accountancy).
- Professional Preparation: CA Foundation (structured preparation in accounting, economics, and business laws for aspiring Chartered Accountants).
- Languages: Mandatory I Language English; II Language Kannada or Hindi (plus Sanskrit/French at Whitefield).
- Focus: Business concepts, analytical reasoning, numerical accuracy, and computer applications for careers in commerce, corporate management, and chartered accountancy.

## Admission
- Public eligibility statement: passed Karnataka SSLC or equivalent recognised by Department of Pre-University Education, Karnataka, and meets prescribed criteria.
- Published admission checklist: 10th TC, marks card, hall-ticket copy, applicable caste/income certificate, applicable eligibility/migration certificates, study certificate, student/parent Aadhaar copies, five white-background passport photos, PU Board application, enrollment form, transport form if applicable, medical certificate if applicable.
- Admissions for 2026-27 are currently open for both North Campus and Whitefield Campus.
