# Gopalan Pre-University College, Whitefield — Rules & Regulations

Source: https://www.gopalancolleges.com/gpuc/rules-regulations.html (crawled 28 October 2026).

## Code of Conduct
- Each student shall conduct herself/himself, both inside and outside the campus of the institute, in a manner befitting a student of a prestigious institute.
- Each student should show due respect and courtesy to the teachers, administrators, staff of the institute, visitors and fellow students.
- The following constitute violation of the code of conduct: lack of courtesy and decorum; wilful damage to institute property; removal of any property belonging to the institute or fellow students; use of abusive and offensive language; disturbing fellow students in their studies; adoption of unfair practices in tests, quizzes, assignments or examinations; noisy and unruly behaviour.
- Talking loudly, loitering or congregating, and being a source of distraction and annoyance to others is not permitted.
- Students are prohibited from taking part in political activity or any activity which is detrimental to the dignity of the institution.

## Dress Code and ID Cards
- Students are required to be dressed neatly and decently.
- Students must always have their ID cards with them and show them on demand to any faculty member or official of the institute.

## Mobile Phone Policy
- The use of cell phones is strictly prohibited in all academic areas of the campus.
- Use of a cell phone will lead to confiscation of the handset.
- A confiscated phone is returned only (a) at the end of the course, or (b) after payment of a fine of Rs. 1,000.

## Attendance and Punctuality
- Students who report late will be recorded as ABSENT for the day.
- Students are not allowed to leave the institute during working hours without the written permission of the mentor/HOD and the Principal.
- Candidates have to fulfil the minimum attendance requirements as per the university norms.
- No student may remain absent from any lecture without leave approved in advance by the class in-charge, and only for a genuine cause.
- A student who is irregular in attendance, fails to show progress, or is not diligent in studies is liable to have his/her name struck off the rolls of the institute.

## Late Joining and Provisional Admission
- For a candidate who joined late because of a late declaration of results, or who took admission provisionally pending the declaration of the lower examination results, lecture requirements will be calculated as per academic rules and provisions.
- If the results are not submitted on time, the admission is subject to cancellation.

## Academic Progress and Discipline
- A student neglecting his/her studies and showing unsatisfactory progress will be given two warnings. If he/she fails to improve after that, he/she will be liable for expulsion from the institute.

## Compulsory Functions
- Students must attend functions and activities organised by the institute on occasions such as Independence Day, Republic Day and Annual Day, whether the function falls on a working day or a holiday.
- Absence from such functions without valid reasons will invite disciplinary action.

## Use of Facilities
- Grounds and sports facilities must be used only under the supervision of the Physical Education Instructor or a teacher.
- Use of the canteen is allowed only during break and lunch timings.
- Students must make all possible efforts to conserve electricity and water, and switch off lights and fans when leaving the classroom, hostel room, lab, etc.

## Vehicles
- Two-wheelers and four-wheelers are not permitted to PUC students.

## Notices
- Students are advised to check the notices displayed on the institute and hostel notice boards regularly. A notice displayed on these boards is deemed to have been served to the students.

## Documents
- All photocopies of required certificates must be produced at the time of admission, and original documents must be furnished if demanded by the institution for office purposes.
- As per PU norms, failure to submit any of the required documents will result in cancellation of admission.
