# Rules and Regulations: Gopalan Pre-University College, Yelahanka (North Campus)

Source: https://gopalanpucnorth.com/rules-and-regulations/ (crawled 28 October 2026).

## Code of Conduct
- Each student must conduct herself or himself, inside and outside the campus, in a manner befitting a student of a prestigious institute, and show due respect and courtesy to teachers, administrators, staff, visitors and fellow students.
- Violations of the code of conduct include: lack of courtesy and decorum, wilful damage to institute property, removing property of the institute or of fellow students, abusive or offensive language, disturbing fellow students in their studies, unfair practices in tests, quizzes, assignments or examinations, and noisy or unruly behaviour.
- Talking loudly, loitering or congregating, or being a source of distraction and annoyance to others is not permitted.
- Students are prohibited from taking part in political activity or any activity detrimental to the dignity of the institution.

## Dress Code and ID Cards
- Students must be dressed neatly and decently.
- Students must always carry their ID cards and show them on demand to any faculty member or official of the institute.

## Mobile Phone Policy
- Use of cell phones is strictly prohibited in all academic areas of the campus.
- A phone used in these areas will be confiscated and returned only (a) at the end of the course, or (b) after payment of a fine of Rs. 1,000.

## Attendance and Punctuality
- Students reporting late are marked ABSENT for the day.
- Students may not leave the institute during working hours without written permission from the mentor or HOD and the Principal.
- Students must meet the minimum attendance requirements as per university norms. No student may be absent from lectures without leave approved in advance by the class in-charge, and only for a genuine reason.
- A student who is irregular in attendance, fails to show progress or is not diligent in studies is liable to have their name struck off the rolls.
- For a student who joined late because of a late declaration of results, or who was admitted provisionally pending results, lecture requirements are calculated as per academic rules. If results are not submitted on time, the admission is subject to cancellation.

## Academic Progress
- A student who neglects studies and shows unsatisfactory progress will be given two warnings. If there is no improvement after that, the student is liable for expulsion.

## Functions and Activities
- Attendance is compulsory at functions and activities organised by the institute on occasions such as Independence Day, Republic Day and Annual Day, whether they fall on a working day or a holiday.
- Absence from such functions without a valid reason will invite disciplinary action.

## Campus Facilities Use
- Students must conserve electricity and water and switch off lights and fans when leaving a classroom, lab or other room.
- The ground and sports facilities may be used only under the supervision of the Physical Education Instructor or a teacher.
- The canteen may be used only during break and lunch timings.
- Two-wheelers and four-wheelers are not permitted for PUC students.

## Notices
- Students should regularly read the notices displayed on the institute notice boards. A notice displayed on these boards is deemed to have been served to the students.

## Admission Document Rules
- Photocopies of all required certificates must be produced at the time of admission, and the original documents must be furnished if the institution asks for them for office purposes.
- As per PU norms, failure to submit any of the required documents will result in cancellation of admission.
