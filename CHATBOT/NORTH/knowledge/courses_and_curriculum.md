# Courses and Curriculum: Gopalan Pre-University College, Yelahanka (North Campus)

Sources: https://gopalanpucnorth.com/courses-offered/, https://gopalanpucnorth.com/science-courses/, https://gopalanpucnorth.com/commerce-courses/, https://gopalanpucnorth.com/faqs/ (crawled 28 October 2026).

## Programme Overview
- Gopalan PU College, Yelahanka offers two-year Pre-University courses (1st PUC and 2nd PUC) in two streams: Science and Commerce.
- Courses in both streams follow the Karnataka Pre-University Education Board curriculum and comply with state board standards for Classes 11 and 12.
- Teaching combines interactive classroom sessions with active learning and practical lab exposure, focusing on conceptual clarity, critical thinking and real understanding.

## Science Stream
- The Science stream builds strong conceptual foundations, analytical reasoning and academic discipline, for students aiming at engineering, medicine and pure sciences.
- It integrates structured teaching with practical laboratory learning to support board examinations and competitive entrance preparation.
- Science combinations:
  * PCMB: Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics, Biology
  * PCMC: Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics, Computer Science
- The Science curriculum includes classroom teaching plus hands-on laboratory training in well-equipped labs.
- Computer Science is offered as a core optional subject in the PCMC combination.

## Science Integrated Coaching
- Integrated coaching is designed to complement the PU curriculum and strengthen competitive exam readiness.
- NEET coaching for PCMB students (NEET Integrated Program): integrates the Physics, Chemistry and Biology syllabus with intensive NEET preparation for medical entrance exams.
- JEE coaching for PCMC students (JEE Integrated Program): combines the PCMC curriculum with focused coaching for JEE Main and Advanced for engineering entrance exams.
- KCET coaching for PCMB and PCMC students (CET Integrated Program): CET coaching alongside regular PCMB or PCMC classes, for students targeting state-level engineering and medical colleges.
- Science students are also trained for the Inspire Award, a national-level science competition.
- The Science programme emphasises a strong reading habit, problem-solving and conceptual clarity as foundations for exams such as CET, NEET and engineering entrances.

## Commerce Stream
- The Commerce stream builds strong foundations in business concepts, analytical reasoning and numerical accuracy, for careers in commerce, management, accounting and economics.
- The programme emphasises conceptual clarity, disciplined study and practical application.
- Commerce combination offered on all course pages:
  * CEBA: Computer Science, Economics, Business Studies, Accountancy
- Computer Science is offered as a core optional subject in the CEBA combination.

## Commerce Integrated Coaching
- CA Foundation Integrated: blends the Commerce stream with CA Foundation coaching (accounting, economics and business laws), building a base for students who plan to become Chartered Accountants, starting from the first year.

## Languages Offered
- I Language: English (mandatory) for all students.
- II Language: Kannada, Hindi or Sanskrit.
- The same language options are listed for both Science and Commerce.

## Value-Added Courses
- The website does not list separate value-added or certificate courses.
- Published add-ons are the integrated coaching programmes (NEET, JEE, KCET, CA Foundation) and Inspire Award training.

## Career Paths After PU
- Science: Engineering, Medicine, Research, Technology, Pure Sciences and allied fields.
- Commerce: Chartered Accountancy (CA Foundation), Company Secretary (CS), Cost Accounting (ICWA), Business and Management undergraduate degrees and related professional programmes.
