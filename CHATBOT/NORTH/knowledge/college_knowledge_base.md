## Courses Offered
- Two streams: Science and Commerce, for 1st PUC and 2nd PUC under the Karnataka PU Board.
- Science combinations:
  * PCMB: Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics, Biology
  * PCMC: Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics, Computer Science
- Commerce combination:
  * CEBA: Computer Science, Economics, Business Studies, Accountancy
- Languages:
  * I Language: English (mandatory)
  * II Language: Kannada, Hindi or Sanskrit
- Integrated coaching (Science): NEET coaching for PCMB students, JEE (Main and Advanced) coaching for PCMC students, and KCET coaching for PCMB and PCMC students.
- Integrated coaching (Commerce): CA Foundation coaching, building a base for Chartered Accountancy from the first year.
- Science students are also trained for the Inspire Award, a national-level science competition.
- Value-added courses: the website does not list separate value-added courses. Please contact the help desk for details.
- Full course details are in the Courses and Curriculum section.

## Campus Facilities
- Digital learning: classrooms have Senses Intelligent Interactive Panels (smart boards) with LED display, multi-touch, Wi-Fi and Bluetooth connectivity.
- Library: fully automated library with print and electronic resources (books, CDs, DVDs, journals), national and international journals, magazines and daily newspapers. It serves over 500 students and staff, has a reading hall for 100 users, and uses N-List Library Management Software.
- Wi-Fi: secure campus-wide Wi-Fi for academic activities and digital learning.
- Laboratories: well-equipped laboratories for hands-on practicals in a safe, modern environment.
- Teaching aids: models, charts, maps, projectors and real-life objects.
- Sports: outdoor cricket ground with practice pitches; outdoor soccer ground with synthetic turf; table tennis in a spacious basement facility; indoor games such as chess and carrom.
- Canteen: the college rules mention a canteen that may be used only during break and lunch timings. No further details are published.

## Admission Eligibility
- Applicants must have passed the Karnataka SSLC examination or any equivalent examination recognised by the Department of Pre-University Education, Karnataka.
- Candidates must also meet all other eligibility criteria prescribed by the department.
- Admission is granted solely at the discretion of Gopalan Pre-University College.

## Admission Process
1. Collect the application form for the two-year Pre-University course from the college office (an online enquiry form is also available on the website).
2. Submit the filled form with the required documents in person at the admission cell, or send it by courier to the college address.
3. Shortlisted candidates are notified and invited to visit the campus to complete admission formalities.
4. A parent or guardian confirms admission by paying the prescribed fees within two days of selection.

## Admission Documents
- The website does not publish a document checklist. Please ask the help desk for the current list.

- As per PU norms, failure to submit any required document will result in cancellation of admission.
