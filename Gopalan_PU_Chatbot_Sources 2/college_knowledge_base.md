## Institution

- Group: Gopalan Group of Institutions.
- Published positioning: academic foundations, experiential learning, critical thinking, discipline, values, character, leadership, holistic growth and preparation for higher education.

## Vision and mission
Vision: develop confident, competent and values-driven individuals; support curiosity, ethical leadership, lifelong learning, academic rigour, personal growth and character.
Mission: provide rigorous and future-focused education combining academic learning, experiential learning, contemporary pedagogy, mentorship and holistic development.

## Science
- Combinations: PCMB (Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics, Biology) and PCMC (Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics, Computer Science).
- Integrated Coaching: NEET Coaching (for PCMB), JEE Coaching (for PCMB & PCMC), and KCET Coaching (for PCMB & PCMC).
- Languages: Mandatory I Language English; II Language Kannada, Hindi, Sanskrit, or French.
- Focus: Strong conceptual foundations, analytical reasoning, and practical laboratory learning for engineering, medicine, and pure sciences.

## Commerce
- Combinations: CEBA (Computer Science, Economics, Business Studies, Accountancy), SEBA (Statistics, Economics, Business Studies, Accountancy), and MEBA (Mathematics, Economics, Business Studies, Accountancy).
- Professional Preparation: CA Foundation (structured preparation in accounting, economics, and business laws for aspiring Chartered Accountants).
- Languages: Mandatory I Language English; II Language Kannada, Hindi, Sanskrit, or French.
- Focus: Business concepts, analytical reasoning, and numerical accuracy for careers in commerce, management, and accounting.

## Admission
- Public eligibility statement: passed Karnataka SSLC or equivalent recognised by Department of Pre-University Education, Karnataka, and meets prescribed criteria.
- Published admission checklist: 10th TC, marks card, hall-ticket copy, applicable caste/income certificate, applicable eligibility/migration certificates, study certificate, student/parent Aadhaar copies, five white-background passport photos, PU Board application, enrollment form, transport form if applicable, medical certificate if applicable.
- Do not decide individual eligibility, promise a seat, or invent deadlines/cut-offs. Route to Admissions.
- Do not collect Aadhaar, caste/religion, income, blood group or family details through the general chatbot lead-capture flow. Those fields appear in a legacy PDF and are not necessary for a general inquiry; any official admission application must use an approved secure process.
