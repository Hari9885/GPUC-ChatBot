## Indexed form contents
- Header: “GOPALAN PRE-UNIVERSITY COLLEGE”, described as approved by Director of Pre University Education, Government of Karnataka.
- Form title: “ENQUIRY FORM / ADMISSION FORM”; academic-year blank and enquiry-number blank.
- Older Science codes: PCMB and “PCMCs”.
- Older Commerce codes in one indexed extract: EBACs, EBABm, EBAS; these differ from current website combinations CEBA, SEBA and MEBA.
- Language choices shown: Kannada, Sanskrit, Hindi, French.
- Candidate fields listed: name, gender, date of birth, religion, caste, category, mother tongue, annual income, blood group.
- Parents' names, occupation and contact details.
- Residential address, last school attended, SSLC/CBSE/ICSE/other registration number, Class X marks and percentages.
- Referral source options including friends, advertisement, peer, current GPUC student, staff and others.
- Declaration that information is true and correct.
- Indexed terms state admission/seat-booking fees paid are non-refundable and that discontinuing mid-course requires payment of two years' course fees.

## Safety and currency
The indexed PDF appears old (search-index timestamp about six years old); it differs from the current HTML admissions page, including course codes, number of photographs and document list. No fee amount is verified from this source. Do not use it to answer current admissions, fee, refund or policy questions until the college confirms it remains valid.

The general-purpose chatbot must not collect religion, caste, blood group, income, Aadhaar or parents' personal details just because those fields were present in an old form. The official admission application, if these fields are still needed, should be handled through an approved and secure process with appropriate notice and legal review.
