Overview: Value Added Courses

Designed to complement the core curriculum, these programmes equip students with practical skills, early-career exposure, and a competitive edge in higher education and professional pathways.

NATA Coaching

Focused preparation for the National Aptitude Test in Architecture, building foundational skills in drawing, spatial reasoning, and architectural aptitude.

Data Analytics with Advanced Excel

Hands-on training in spreadsheet analysis, data organisation, and reporting skills essential for commerce, management, and analytical roles.

Tally

Practical exposure to accounting software fundamentals, enabling students to understand financial records, bookkeeping, and business accounting processes.

Introduction to Data Science

An entry-level programme introducing data concepts, basic analytics, and logical thinking, preparing students for technology and data-driven fields.

Coursera (Online Certification Courses)

Curated online certification courses from leading universities and industry experts, helping students build global skills, explore diverse domains, and enhance academic and career readiness.
