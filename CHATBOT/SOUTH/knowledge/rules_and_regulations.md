# Gopalan Pre-University College — Rules & Regulations

**Campus:** Whitefield Campus, Bengaluru  
**Published Source:** https://www.gopalancolleges.com/gpuc/rules-regulations.html

---

## 1. General Conduct & Decorum
- Each student shall conduct herself/himself, both inside and outside the campus of the institute, in a manner befitting a student of a prestigious educational institution.
- Each student should show due respect and courtesy to the teachers, administrators, staff of the institute, visitors, and fellow students.
- **Violations of Code of Conduct:**
  - Lack of courtesy and decorum.
  - Willful damage to institute property.
  - Removal of any property belonging to the Institute or fellow students.
  - Use of abusive and offensive language.
  - Disturbing fellow students in their studies.
  - Adoption of unfair practices in tests, quizzes, assignments, or examinations.
  - Noisy and unruly behavior.
- Talking loudly, loitering or congregating, and being a source of distraction or annoyance to others is strictly prohibited.

---

## 2. Dress Code & Identity Cards
- Students are required to be dressed neatly and decently in accordance with institutional decorum.
- Students must always carry their official **College Identity Cards** with them and produce the same on demand by any faculty member or official of the institute.

---

## 3. Cell Phone Prohibition Policy (Strict)
- The use of cell phones is **strictly prohibited** in all academic areas of the campus.
- Any unauthorized use of cell phones entails immediate **confiscation of the handset**.
- Confiscated phones will be returned only under either of the following two conditions:
  1. **At the end of the two-year PU course**, OR
  2. **After payment of a penalty fine of Rs. 1,000/-**.

---

## 4. Attendance, Punctuality & Campus Movement
- **Late Reporting:** Students who report late will be recorded as **ABSENT** for the entire day.
- **Campus Movement:** Students are **not allowed to leave the institute premises during working hours** without the prior written permission of the designated Mentor/HOD and the Principal.
- **Board Attendance Norms:** Candidates must fulfill the minimum attendance requirements prescribed by the Department of Pre-University Education / University norms.
- **Leave Approval:** No student may remain absent from any lectures without their leave having been approved previously by the class in-charge for a genuine cause.
- **Roll Strikethrough:** If a student is irregular in attendance or fails to show progress and diligence in studies, their name is liable to be struck off from the rolls of the institute.

---

## 5. Academic Negligence & Disciplinary Warnings
- A student neglecting his/her studies and demonstrating unsatisfactory academic progress will be given **two formal warnings**.
- If the student fails to improve after two warnings, he/she will be liable for **expulsion from the institute**.

---

## 6. Mandatory Attendance on Institutional & National Functions
- It is compulsory for all students to attend functions and activities organized by the institute on special occasions, including:
  - **Independence Day (15th August)**
  - **Republic Day (26th January)**
  - **Annual Day Celebrations**
- Attendance is mandatory whether the function falls on a regular working day or a declared holiday. Absence from such functions without a valid approved reason invites disciplinary action.

---

## 7. Vehicles Policy (Strict Prohibition)
- **Two-wheelers or four-wheelers are strictly NOT permitted** for PUC students within or around the college campus.
- Students are encouraged to utilize college bus transportation or public transit.

---

## 8. Facility Usage Guidelines
- **Canteen:** Use of the college canteen is permitted **only during official short break and lunch timings**. Students are not permitted in the cafeteria during lecture hours.
- **Sports Grounds:** College grounds or sports facilities must be utilized strictly under the direct supervision of the Physical Education Instructor or assigned teacher.
- **Resource Conservation:** Students must make all possible efforts to conserve electricity and water. They must switch off lights and fans upon leaving classrooms, laboratories, or communal spaces.

---

## 9. Notice Board Communications
- Students are advised to regularly inspect official notices displayed on the institute and hostel notice boards.
- Any notice displayed on these boards shall be deemed to have been legally and officially served to the students.

---

## 10. Document Submission & Verification
- All photocopies of required qualifying certificates must be produced at the time of admission.
- Original documents must be furnished whenever demanded by the institution for verification with the Pre-University Board.
- **As per PU Board norms, failure to submit any mandatory qualifying documents will result in cancellation of admission.**
